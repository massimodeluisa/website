[Open Bio Page](https://openbio.page/) puts that page on a domain you already control. The home is the people. Open one of them and you are on their page, with the portrait, the line, and the links they chose to hand out, written the way they want to be seen. A site, an email, a profile, or any other link you put there.

I made the first directory for my family. This is that directory with the names taken out, so the next family or the next team can put their own in.

The [docs home](https://openbio.page/) has a prompt that publishes one for you. Choose Family or personal, or Company or team, then copy it or open it in Claude Code, Codex, Cursor, Claude, or ChatGPT. The agent asks once for the hostname and for each person, keeps the wording you give it, and publishes the site. If there is no domain, it stops. The same steps, if you want to do them yourself, are at [Create your bio](https://openbio.page/create).

A later login edits only that person's page. Visitors do not need one.
